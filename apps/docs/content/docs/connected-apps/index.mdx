---
title: Connected apps
description: Connect accounts and grant bots access
---

Open **Plugins → Connected apps** on desktop or **Connected Apps** in Expo.
Choose a native integration or **Add remote MCP integration**, then press
**Connect**. The screen offers a sign-in link, credential form or QR pairing
instructions according to the integration.

Native integrations currently include Telegram, Slack, Discord and WhatsApp
for text-message sending. Remote MCP integrations expose the provider's tool
schemas and actions through OpenClaw.

After connecting, approve the exact account for each bot. Additional accounts
need distinct labels; an integration can have up to five accounts. Bot tool
actions still follow the bot's approval policy.

OpenClaw runs on your server and owns the provider credentials. No provider
tokens are sent to the OpenClaw company or inserted into bot prompts.

Read [OpenClaw runtime](./openclaw.mdx) for hosting, OAuth callbacks, QR pairing
and the pinned adapter boundary.
